## Brand & Style

This design system defines an ultra-luxury, daylight-informed aesthetic tailored for premier legal practitioners, family offices, and high-stakes dispute resolution. It translates executive authority, institutional integrity, and confidential precision into an illuminating, tactile daylight environment.

The visual direction marries **Warm Editorial Minimalism** with **Light Glassmorphism**:
- Soft alabaster and warm ivory foundations replace stark sterile whites, evoking heavyweight vellum, bespoke parchment, and sunlit private chambers.
- Luminous champagne and satin gold micro-gradations replace flat digital colors, lending depth to key interactions and navigational touchpoints.
- Deep, obsidian and rich espresso typographic contrasts preserve editorial rigor, ensuring sustained reading comfort across lengthy legal briefs and evidentiary records.
- Tactile glass surfaces leverage high-diffusion backdrops and ultra-fine metallic hairlines, imbuing every docket card, affidavit drawer, and status chip with the tangible quality of handcrafted crystal and precious metal.

## Colors

The daylight palette balances radiant warmth with deep, authoritative contrast. Surfaces reject unpigmented stark whites in favor of soft natural vellums, complemented by rich ink tones and multi-faceted gold metals.

- **Foundational Surfaces:** 
  - `canvas-ivory` (`#fbfaf7`) serves as the base viewport backdrop, providing an eye-resting, warm foundation.
  - `surface-parchment` (`#f6f3eb`) defines secondary panels, sub-canvas divisions, and inactive wells.
  - `surface-glass` (`rgba(251, 250, 247, 0.78)`) forms the baseline for frosted cards, floating headers, and docket tiles.
- **Accents & Precious Metals:**
  - `gold-burnished` (`#c59b27`) is the primary active color, ensuring WCAG AA compliant contrast against light grounds for critical text, active tabs, and focused outlines.
  - `gold-champagne` (`#d4af37`) powers lustrous gradient blends, badge surfaces, and primary button hover rings.
  - `gold-highlight` (`#f5e8be`) provides soft interior halos, wash fills, and active item indicators.
- **Typography & High-Contrast Anchors:**
  - `ink-obsidian` (`#181716`) delivers supreme readability for authoritative headlines, case names, and primary action text.
  - `ink-espresso` (`#2d2a26`) anchors body paragraphs, metadata labels, and form fields.
  - `ink-muted` (`#757065`) handles secondary timestamps, docket IDs, and non-blocking subtext.
- **Borders & Dividers:**
  - Micro-borders use `border-gold-subtle` (`rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.16)`) and `border-vellum` (`rgba(45, 42, 38, 0.07)`) to define spatial volume without rigid lines.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes classical jurisprudence with contemporary precision:

- **Noto Serif (Display & Headlines):** Infuses every case file, brief title, and judicial action with gravitas and editorial permanence. Optical sizing leans towards refined, deliberate weights (500 to 600) to ensure high legibility against pearl and ivory backdrops.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Operational & Body Copy):** Offers crisp, contemporary clarity for high-density judicial dashboards, evidence ledgers, and collaborative notes. Renders smoothly at smaller points without character deterioration.
- **Letter Spacing & Character Case:** Display sizes leverage subtle negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) to bind headline rhythm. Microcopy and labels incorporate wider positive tracking (`+0.04em` to `+0.08em`) and small caps for docket entries, dates, and status signifiers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8px modular cadence structured around clean editorial columns:

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - Mobile (under 768px): 4-column fluid layout with `1.25rem` screen margins and `0.75rem` gutters.
  - Tablet (768px – 1024px): 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters.
  - Desktop (1024px+): 12-column structured layout capped at a max-width of `1440px`, centered with `2rem` outer margins.
- **Spatial Rhythm:**
  - Component interiors rely strictly on `space-sm` (8px), `space-md` (16px), and `space-lg` (24px) for coherent density.
  - Generous section boundaries (`space-xl` / 36px) grant sensitive legal matters a composed, unhurried presence.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through daylight transparency, radiant gold refractions, and soft ambient diffusion rather than heavy physical drop shadows:

- **Light Glassmorphism:** Primary cards employ `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` over `rgba(251, 250, 247, 0.78)`. Floating navigation bars, active sheets, and modal sheets elevate to `backdrop-filter: blur(28px)` with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)`, producing luminous optical diffusion over underlying dockets.
- **Micro-Gold Hairline Borders:** Cards and elevated panes feature a dual-gradient 1px perimeter:
  - Default: `linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.28) 0%, rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08) 50%, rgba(45, 42, 38, 0.04) 100%)`.
  - Focused / Active: `linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.55) 0%, rgba(245, 232, 190, 0.4) 100%)`.
- **Sunlit Ambient Shadows:**
  - Ambient Lift: `0 8px 30px -4px rgba(45, 42, 38, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(45, 42, 38, 0.02)`.
  - Aureate Focus: `0 10px 32px -4px rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.12), 0 0 16px 1px rgba(245, 232, 190, 0.4)`.

## Shapes

The geometric framework (`roundedness: 2`) maintains an architectonic balance between structural legal discipline and ergonomic softness:

- Interactive inputs, standard buttons, and tab segments feature a base `0.5rem` (8px) radius.
- Glassmorphic case files, timeline containers, and briefing cards use `1rem` (16px) corners (`rounded-lg`).
- Modal sheets, document envelopes, and multi-tier drawers employ `1.5rem` (24px) corners (`rounded-xl`).
- Micro-tags, evidentiary status chips, and toggle caps retain a continuous pill silhouette (`9999px`) to visually differentiate metadata from content blocks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Champagne Aurum):** Gradient fill shifting from `#d4af37` to `#c59b27`. Typography set in `#181716` at weight 600. Finished with a top inner highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.45)`) and an exterior warm glow `0 4px 16px rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.2)`.
- **Secondary (Frosted Pearl):** Translucent fill `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)` with backdrop blur (12px) and a subtle perimeter `rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.24)`. Text set in `#2d2a26`. On hover, the fill shifts to `rgba(245, 232, 190, 0.35)`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Transparent background with `#c59b27` typography and an interactive underline or directional chevron on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips:** Height 26px, pill contour. Background in `rgba(246, 243, 235, 0.85)` with a 1px border of `rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.2)`. Text set in `#2d2a26` (weight 600, tracked +0.04em).
- **Evidentiary State Lights:** Embedded 6px luminous jewel dot:
  - Active / Hearing: Radiant gold `#c59b27` with ambient corona `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)`.
  - Concluded / Filed: Olive slate `#4d5548`.
  - Critical / Urgent: Rich carmine `#8b261e`.

### Input Fields
- Well surface in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)` framed by a 1px border of `rgba(45, 42, 38, 0.1)`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.6)` accompanied by an ambient halo `0 0 0 3px rgba(245, 232, 190, 0.45)`. Text sits in `#181716`, while placeholder text remains in `#aaa395`.

### Lists & Docket Logs
- Glass rows utilizing alternating light tints (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.45)` to `rgba(246, 243, 235, 0.65)`) separated by 1px dividers of `rgba(45, 42, 38, 0.05)`.
- Leading item: Docket case hash in monospaced formatting encapsulated in a micro-pearl badge with gold perimeter.
- Trailing item: High-contrast state signifier or timestamp in `#757065`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Micro-rounded containers framed with 1.5px solid `rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.35)` over a pearl white bed.
- Checked state: Filled with the signature gold gradient (`#d4af37` to `#c59b27`), displaying an obsidian icon with a soft ambient gold shadow.

### Cards & Case Modules
- Structured in `surface-glass` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`.
- Top boundary carries a luminous edge line: `linear-gradient(90deg, transparent, rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35), transparent)`.
- Interior sections utilize `space-md` or `space-lg` padding to ensure briefs and evidentiary tables maintain generous breathing room.
